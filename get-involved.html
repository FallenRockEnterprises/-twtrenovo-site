Why Your Support Matters

The Stronghold at Renovo is a hybrid model that combines a 501(c)(3) nonprofit ministry (The Welcoming Table) with a for-profit business venture (Fallen Rock Enterprises). Your support helps sustain:

  • Free or donation-based community meals for anyone in Renovo
  • Recovery support groups and peer-led programming
  • One-on-one resource navigation and spiritual care
  • Safe, youth-friendly recreational spaces and gaming events
  • Facilities that serve as a welcoming gathering place for the entire community

Volunteer Opportunities

The Welcoming Table relies on passionate volunteers to make community gatherings, meals, and support circles possible. Here are ways to serve:

Community Meals

Help prepare, cook, or serve nutritious meals to the community. Whether you have culinary experience or simply want to help, all skill levels are welcome.

Peer Support Facilitation

If you have lived experience in recovery and are interested in supporting others, we offer training for peer facilitators. Contact Todd and Mandi to learn more about this role.

Administrative & Outreach

Help with scheduling, communications, resource coordination, and community outreach. This could include phone calls, email coordination, or event planning.

Gaming & Youth Events

Assist with setup, facilitation, and supervision of Magic, D&D, and other tabletop gaming nights. Great for those passionate about youth engagement.

Transportation & Accessibility

Provide rides for participants who lack reliable transportation, or help ensure our spaces meet accessibility needs for people with disabilities.

Professional Skills & In-Kind Donations

Do you have expertise in graphic design, accounting, grant writing, or another field? We welcome pro-bono professional services and donations of supplies, furniture, or equipment.

Ready to volunteer? Contact Todd & Mandi to discuss how your gifts and availability can make an impact.

Other Ways to Support

  • Attend Events: Show up for community meals, gaming nights, or worship gatherings. Your presence is community.
  • Spread the Word: Share our mission with friends, family, and colleagues. Word-of-mouth is powerful.
  • Connect Resources: Know a landlord, contractor, or service provider who believes in this work? Make an introduction.
  • Advocate Locally: Support zoning approvals, attend city meetings, and encourage local government and business support.
  • Pray & Hold Us in Thought: Spiritual support and intercession are vital. If faith is central to your life, we invite your prayers.

Questions?

If you have questions about giving, volunteering, or any aspect of The Welcoming Table or The Stronghold, reach out directly. Todd and Mandi are accessible and eager to discuss how you can be part of building a movement of healing and hope in Renovo.
