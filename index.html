The Welcoming Table (501c3 Nonprofit)

The Welcoming Table is a Pennsylvania nonprofit corporation (pending 501c3 status) that will own and operate a community hub at TBD, Renovo, PA. The organization serves as the mission-driven landlord and social service provider within the hybrid business model, with donations and contributions funding all community programs.

Mission and Purpose

The Welcoming Table's core mission is to provide sanctuary, community connection, nutritional support, and resource navigation to individuals in the Central Pennsylvania region, fostering recovery and holistic well-being.

The organization addresses a critical need in Renovo, which has experienced decades of population loss and economic decline, creating a shortage of safe "third places" where community members can gather.

Year 1 Programs (Funded by Contributions)

The Welcoming Table will operate four distinct program areas:

Community Meals Program

  • Free or donation-based community meals offered 2–3 times per week
  • Prepared in an on-site commercial kitchen with professional health certification
  • Accessible to all residents of Renovo and surrounding areas regardless of economic status or faith background
  • Addresses immediate food insecurity while creating a gathering space

Recovery Support and Peer Programs

  • Peer support meetings focused on recovery and wellness
  • Facilitated by trained peer specialists with lived experience in recovery
  • Provides a safe, stigma-free environment for individuals seeking support
  • Led by Todd M. Lynch (Georgia Certified Peer Specialist) and Amanda Lynch (with decades of lived experience in resource navigation)

Resource Navigation Services

  • Professional guidance connecting individuals to county and state resources
  • Specialized support for mental health challenges, addiction recovery, and housing assistance
  • "Warm handoff" model that actively bridges individuals to necessary social services
  • Available by appointment in dedicated office space

Supervised Gaming Nights

  • Friday Night Magic and Dungeons & Dragons sessions
  • Youth-focused and family-friendly activities
  • Creates safe recreational space for community engagement
  • Jointly hosted with Fallen Rock Entertainment DBA

Future Expansion Programs

As the organization stabilizes and raises additional funds, The Welcoming Table plans to expand services to include:

  • Daily meals program
  • Youth mentorship initiatives
  • Catering services for community events
  • Substance-free event hosting for regional gatherings

Organizational Governance

To ensure proper 501c3 compliance and legal integrity, The Welcoming Table operates under strict nonprofit governance:

  • 5-Member Board of Directors comprising Amanda Lynch (President), Todd Lynch (Secretary), a Treasurer, and two independent At-Large directors
  • Formal Conflict of Interest Policy requiring that any lease or transaction with Fallen Rock Enterprises, LLC be approved exclusively by independent directors
  • Annual Fair-Market-Value Review of the $1,500/month lease with Fallen Rock Enterprises
  • Mission-First Allocation: All net rent income is directed back into meals, programs, and facility maintenance rather than distributed to individuals
  • Public Accessibility: All programs are open to the public regardless of faith background or economic status

The Stronghold at Renovo

The Stronghold at Renovo is the overall project name for the community hub located at TBD, Renovo, PA. It represents the physical venue and operational ecosystem combining The Welcoming Table's mission-driven programs with Fallen Rock Enterprises' revenue-generating business operations.

Project Overview

The Stronghold transforms a historic church building into a safe "third place" offering:

  • Community meals and sanctuary space
  • Recovery support and peer navigation
  • Youth and adult gaming tournaments
  • E-commerce and local retail operations
  • Professional consulting and resource linkage services

The project targets a launch date of April 1, 2026 with an $85,000 capital raise.

Business Lines (DBAs Operating from The Stronghold)

Fallen Rock Enterprises, LLC operates five distinct business divisions from the shared facility:

  1. Fallen Rock Collectibles & Consignments – High-volume e-commerce resale via eBay, specializing in trading cards, vintage goods, and collectibles (sourced through exclusive partnership with LCJ Services)
  2. The Stronghold (Venue Operations) – Hosts events, tournaments, community meals, and public gatherings
  3. Fallen Rock Entertainment – Tabletop gaming tournaments (Magic: The Gathering, Dungeons & Dragons, Warhammer 40K) and creative intellectual property development (books, screenplays)
  4. Fallen Rock Resources – Recovery coaching, resource navigation, peer support, and "warm handoff" to county services
  5. Fallen Rock Properties – Future expansion into affordable and second-chance housing programs throughout the region

Contribution-Funded Programs at The Stronghold

Contributions to The Welcoming Table nonprofit fund all mission-based programming at The Stronghold:

  • Community meals (food costs, kitchen operations, supplies)
  • Peer support meeting facilitation and coordinator salaries
  • Resource navigation consultations and materials
  • Gaming equipment and facilities for supervised recreational programming
  • Building maintenance and utilities (covered jointly between rent revenue and donations)

Financial Structure

The Stronghold operates on a "firewall" model separating mission from commerce:

The Welcoming Table (Nonprofit/Landlord)

  • Monthly rental income: $1,500 from Fallen Rock Enterprises
  • Monthly expenses: $1,435 (mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance)
  • Net position: +$65 break-even buffer
  • All mission programs funded by tax-deductible donations and contributions

Fallen Rock Enterprises (For-Profit Tenant)

  • Monthly revenue target: $6,200
    • E-commerce: $3,700 (~6 items/day)
    • Gaming tournaments/events: $1,500
    • Local retail sales: $1,000
  • Monthly expenses: $6,200 (rent $1,500, utilities $800, owner salaries $3,500, other $400)
  • Rent payment to The Welcoming Table covers the nonprofit's mortgage and basic operations
