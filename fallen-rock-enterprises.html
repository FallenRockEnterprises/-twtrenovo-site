Fallen Rock Enterprises, LLC

A mission-aligned business ecosystem operating from The Stronghold at Renovo.

Who We Are

Fallen Rock Enterprises, LLC is the for-profit tenant at The Stronghold at Renovo, operating multiple business divisions from a shared community hub. The company combines e-commerce, events, services, and future housing initiatives with a focus on creating opportunity in Renovo.

While deeply aligned with the vision of The Welcoming Table nonprofit, Fallen Rock maintains clear legal and financial separation. Lease payments, shared space agreements, and coordinated programming are all structured to honor both the mission and the integrity of the firewall between nonprofit and business activity.

How We Support the Mission

  • Lease payments help cover the mortgage and essential operating costs of The Stronghold facility.
  • Business activity creates local work, skill-building, and leadership opportunities for people connected to Renovo.
  • Events and services draw new visitors into the hub, where they can also discover The Welcoming Table’s community programs.
  • Shared planning ensures that business operations and nonprofit programs strengthen one another while respecting clear boundaries.
